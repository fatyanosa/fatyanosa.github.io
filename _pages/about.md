Hi! I’m currently a lecturer at the Faculty of Computer Science (FILKOM), Universitas Brawijaya, where I’ve been teaching since 2023. I earned both my Bachelor’s (S.Kom.) and Master’s (M.Kom.) degrees from Universitas Brawijaya, and later went on to complete my Ph.D. at Kumamoto University in 2021.

During my Ph.D. journey, I had the opportunity to intern at Kata.ai as an AI Research Scientist Intern from 2020 to 2021. After graduating, I continued working as a researcher at the Algorithm Laboratory at Kumamoto University until 2022. I was also involved with the AI Center at Universitas Brawijaya as part of the Research Division from 2023 to 2024.

Before diving into academia, I spent several years in the tech industry, from 2013 to 2018, working with companies like Mitrais and Anomadic as a Software Engineer and Quality Assurance Specialist. These experiences really shaped my perspective and continue to inform my teaching and research today.
