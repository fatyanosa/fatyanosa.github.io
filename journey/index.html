9–10 Sep 2026 Moderator

Moderator, Keynote Speech "Agentic AI yang Tepercaya" by Dr. Eng. Ayu Purwarianti, SENTRIN 2026

Auditorium Algoritma (Gedung G lantai 2 FILKOM), Fakultas Ilmu Komputer, Universitas Brawijaya

Moderated keynote speech at the Seminar Nasional Teknologi & Rekayasa Informasi (SENTRIN) 2026, held 9–10 September 2026, 10.45–11.45 WIB, at Auditorium Algoritma, FILKOM UB. The keynote, Agentic AI yang Tepercaya (Trustworthy Agentic AI), was delivered by Dr. Eng. Ayu Purwarianti, S.T., M.T., an academic and researcher in natural language processing at Institut Teknologi Bandung, former Chair of the Indonesian Association for Computational Linguistics (INACL) and of the IEEE Education Chapter Indonesia Section. The talk covered how LLM-based agents can work autonomously, and the risks and AI governance needed to keep them trustworthy.

8 Sep 2026 Committee

Ruang 4.1, Gedung Kreativitas Mahasiswa (GKM), FILKOM, Universitas Brawijaya

Chaired the organizing committee (ketua panitia) of a guest lecture at FILKOM UB featuring Professor Nakashima Yuta of The University of Osaka.
