3–6 Nov 2026 Certification

Competency Assessor Training and Certification (BNSP)

Laboratorium TIK, Universitas Brawijaya, Malang

Completed the Competency Assessor Training (Pelatihan Asesor Kompetensi, Workplace Assessor) organized by LSP Entrepreneur Digital Indonesia in collaboration with the Indonesian Professional Certification Authority (BNSP), 3–6 November 2026. The training covered four competency units (48 hours in total), including planning assessment activities and processes, conducting assessments, and contributing to assessment validation.

31 Oct 2026 Certification

Competency Assessment Internalization Program (BNSP)

Universitas Brawijaya, Malang

Passed the Competency Assessment Internalization Program (Program Internalisasi Asesmen Kompetensi) organized by LSP Entrepreneur Digital Indonesia, an 8-hour program on competency standards and certification schemes, the assessor code of ethics, and practice mapping job groups.

30 Oct 2026 Exhibition

Exhibition at Living Lab Summit, Bestari Saintek

Universitas Brawijaya

Contributed to Bestari Saintek and exhibited our work at the Living Lab Summit. The exhibition is a collaboration with the Early Childhood Teacher Education Program (Program Studi Pendidikan Guru PAUD, PG-PAUD), Universitas Negeri Semarang (UNNES), under the Faculty of Education and Psychology (Fakultas Ilmu Pendidikan dan Psikologi, FIPP), on building an Immersive Room for understanding disasters for Early Childhood.

5 Oct 2026 Visit

Online Research Discussion with Prof. Chenhui Chu, Kyoto University

Online meeting

Joined an online meeting between FILKOM UB and Prof. Chenhui Chu of Kyoto University to discuss research developments and opportunities for collaboration. Presented the three benchmarks built from NusaCrowd (NusaNLU, NusaNLG, and NusaASR) and ParaCotta, a paraphrase corpus method published at PACLIC 2021. The discussion supports FILKOM UB’s research on Visual Scene-aware Machine Translation.

8 Sep 2026 Committee

Organizing Committee Chair, Guest Lecture "Does a Machine Understand? Explainability and Bias in AI"

Ruang 4.1, Gedung Kreativitas Mahasiswa (GKM), FILKOM, Universitas Brawijaya

Chaired the organizing committee (ketua panitia) of a guest lecture at FILKOM UB featuring Professor Nakashima Yuta of The University of Osaka. The talk explored whether machines truly understand or only simulate understanding (the Turing Test, the Chinese Room argument, and the symbol grounding problem), as well as explainability and bias in AI models.
