**PediaExpert** is a web chatbot for nutritionists and healthcare workers who decide on nutrition therapy for children. It retrieves answers from nutrition guidelines and the scientific literature and cites its sources, so every recommendation can be audited. It is meant to support national efforts against childhood stunting. The product is currently in beta.

I work on the AI side of the project as AI Engineer, within the AI Center at Universitas Brawijaya, together with Universitas Ma Chung.
